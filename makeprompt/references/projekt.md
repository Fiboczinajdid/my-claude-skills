# Prompts for Project instructions

Project instructions are sent with every message in every chat of the Project. Every line costs tokens on every turn and shapes every task, not just one.

- Describe standing behaviour, not a single task: the context and role, who the user is, the recurring kinds of work, preferences for tone and format, what to do when unsure.
- Leave out rules for one-off situations; they distort unrelated chats.
- Keep it short. Rough sizes: Zwięzły 50–100 words, Standard 150–300, Pełny up to about 500. Long reference material belongs in the Project's knowledge files; the instructions can point to them.
- Write in English, which tokenizes more cheaply on every message, and end with an instruction to respond in the user's language.
- With more than a handful of rules, group them under short labels or XML tags.
